Operations Workflow Guide (Sprint 2)

The Human Data Operations Workbench enables rapid review and curation of proctoring observation data without modifying core behavior heuristics or altering raw dataset files on disk.

1. Image Audit & Crops

Audit legacy 707 images. Normalize classes to observable posture labels. Preview actor crops dynamically.

2. Video Timeline & Diff

Annotate millisecond episodes on demo video. Compare Human GT vs AI proposals with Temporal IoU.

3. Spatial Quality Check

Validate 4-point seat polygons, verify writing zones, and detect overlapping desk bounding boxes.

4. Curate ML Dataset

Package reviewed data with group-safe deterministic hashing (no split leakage) and manifest.json.

Observable Label Mapping

Ground-truth must represent observable physical actions, not legal conclusions:

  • NORMAL_WRITING Candidate upright, looking at paper
  • HEAD_TURN_LEFT Head turned >35° toward left neighbor
  • HEAD_TURN_RIGHT Head turned >35° toward right neighbor
  • TORSO_LEAN_RIGHT Upper body leaned across aisle
  • HAND_BELOW_DESK Hand reaching under table or into pocket
  • PHONE_OR_DEVICE Direct device holding or viewing
